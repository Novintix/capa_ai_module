HISTORICAL CAPA RECORD
CAPA No   : CAPA-2024-0033
Product   : Metformin HCl 500mg Film-Coated Tablets
Batch     : B-178
Issue     : Film coat orange-peel texture; batch failed appearance spec at T0.
Opened    : 2024-04-10
Closed    : 2024-06-18

-----------------------------------------------------------
1. INVESTIGATION SUMMARY

Root cause identified: Spray rate set above specification (32 g/min vs. spec max 25 g/min)
for the first 90 minutes of coating. Excess spray rate caused wet coat pooling,
leading to surface texture defects upon drying.

Contributing factor: Operator misread batch record spray rate column (mixed up two gun
configuration vs. single gun configuration). Training record showed operator had not
completed updated batch record training introduced in 2024.

-----------------------------------------------------------
2. CORRECTIVE ACTIONS

CA-01 : Batch B-178 rejected. Disposition: destruction.
CA-02 : Spray rate alarm limits programmed into CP-03 and CP-04 control systems
        (upper limit 26 g/min; hard stop at 30 g/min).
CA-03 : Batch record revised to clearly distinguish single-gun and dual-gun spray rate
        columns with color coding and header notes.

-----------------------------------------------------------
3. PREVENTIVE ACTIONS

PA-01 : All coating operators retrained on revised batch record (SOP-MFG-018 v3.0).
        Training completed by 2024-05-30. Training records filed.
PA-02 : Coaching session conducted with Shift A and Shift B coating teams on alarm
        response expectations per POL-QA-005. Session dated 2024-05-15.
PA-03 : Quarterly coating process parameter review added to site quality calendar.

-----------------------------------------------------------
4. EFFECTIVENESS CHECK

Verification performed 2024-09-01. Review of 4 subsequent coating batches (B-181, B-185,
B-190, B-195): zero spray rate deviations recorded. Appearance results all passing at T0.
CAPA declared effective.
