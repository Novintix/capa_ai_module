STABILITY STUDY REPORT
Product   : Metformin HCl 500mg Film-Coated Tablets
Batch     : B-202
Protocol  : STAB-MET-007
Condition : 40 C / 75% RH (Accelerated)
Timepoint : 3 Months
Report No : SR-2026-0041
Date      : 2026-02-15

-----------------------------------------------------------
1. OBJECTIVE
To evaluate physical and chemical stability of Batch B-202 at the 3-month accelerated
stability timepoint per ICH Q1A guidelines.

-----------------------------------------------------------
2. OBSERVATIONS

2.1 Visual / Appearance
  Initial (T0)   : Smooth, white film coat. No defects observed.
  3 Month (T3)   : Peeling and flaking of film coat observed on approximately 22% of tablets.
                   Cracks noted on coat surface, primarily at tablet edges.
                   Coat adhesion appears weakened.

2.2 Dissolution (USP Apparatus II, 900 mL, 0.1N HCl, 50 RPM, 45 min)
  Spec           : NLT 75% at 45 min
  T0 result      : 91% (average of 6 units)
  T3 result      : 63% (average of 6 units) -- FAIL

2.3 Assay (HPLC)
  Spec           : 95.0 - 105.0%
  T0 result      : 100.2%
  T3 result      : 99.6% -- PASS

2.4 Related Substances
  Spec           : Total impurities NMT 0.5%
  T3 result      : 0.18% -- PASS

2.5 Hardness (at T3)
  Spec           : 8 - 14 kP
  T3 result      : 9.2 kP (average) -- PASS

-----------------------------------------------------------
3. ANALYST COMMENTS

The peeling and cracking of the film coat is the primary finding of concern. Coat failure
is likely contributing to the dissolution failure by exposing uneven tablet surface area
to dissolution media. Chemical stability appears unaffected. Coating process parameters
should be reviewed as the most probable cause of coat integrity failure.

-----------------------------------------------------------
4. CONCLUSION

Batch B-202 FAILS the 3-month accelerated stability criteria.
Failure attributed to film coat defect (peeling/cracking).
Formal investigation and CAPA required per SOP-QA-021.
