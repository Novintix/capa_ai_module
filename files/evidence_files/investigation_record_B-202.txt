1. PROBLEM STATEMENT
Stability report SR-2026-0041 identified film coat peeling on ~22% of tablets and
dissolution failure (63% vs. spec NLT 75%) at the 3-month accelerated stability
timepoint (40 C / 75% RH). Investigation initiated to determine root cause.

-----------------------------------------------------------
2. EVIDENCE GATHERED

- Batch record incomplete: no deviation form filed for temperature or pan speed events.
- Shift A handover section blank.

  continued during excursion.
- Pan speed reduced to 6 RPM at 10:15. Deviation not escalated.
- Written handover not completed at shift change (14:00).
- Shift B operator ran pan at 6 RPM for approximately 2 hours before correcting.

2.3 Process Data Review (pan_speed_temp_B-202.csv)
- Temperature excursion confirmed: 08:28-08:57 (duration ~29 minutes), peak 74.2 C.
- Pan speed at 6 RPM confirmed: 10:14-16:03 (duration ~5 hours, 49 minutes).

-----------------------------------------------------------
4. PRELIMINARY FINDINGS

a. HIGH-TEMPERATURE EXCURSION: Inlet air temperature exceeded specification limit (74 C vs. max
   60 C) for approximately 29 minutes. Elevated temperature may have caused rapid moisture
   evaporation, leading to brittle, poorly adhered coat film.

b. EXTENDED LOW PAN SPEED: Pan speed below specification (6 RPM vs. min 7 RPM) for nearly
   6 hours. Low pan speed reduces tablet-to-tablet movement, causing uneven coat deposition
   and localized thick/thin areas susceptible to peeling.

c. PROCEDURAL NON-COMPLIANCE: SOP-MFG-018 requires immediate escalation of parameter
   deviations and written shift handover. Neither requirement was fulfilled.
