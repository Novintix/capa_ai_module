9. RISK ASSESSMENT

10. CONTAINMENT ACTIONS
- Quarantine retained and unreleased units.
- Expanded visual inspection sampling.
- Temporary requirement: QA witness pre-booking for all post-maintenance restarts.
- Shift briefing issued on written handover requirement.

11. PROPOSED CAPA PLAN
CAPA-1: MES lock to prevent RUN status with blank witness field on FRM-MFG-022.
CAPA-2: Mandatory deviation auto-prompt if restart event occurs before checklist closure.
CAPA-3: Supervisor accountability KPI on open item handover compliance.
CAPA-4: Focused retraining with practical simulation on restart governance.
CAPA-5: Monthly recurrence review with QA, Production, and Engineering.

12. EFFECTIVENESS CRITERIA
- 100 percent post-maintenance restarts with complete witness sign-off before RUN.
- Zero verbal-only transfer of open critical items for 3 consecutive months.
- At least 90 percent reduction in delayed critical entries within 2 quarters.
