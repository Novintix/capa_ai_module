- Scoring dimensions: requirement knowledge, decision timeliness, escalation correctness, documentation timeliness.

7.1 Operator Cohort Findings
- Knowledge scores generally above 85 percent.

- Most common behavior gap: waiting for supervisor or QA confirmation despite clear trigger conditions.

7.2 Supervisor Cohort Findings

7.4 QA Cohort Findings

- Main limitation: resource constraints during concurrent high-priority events.

SECTION 8 - ROOT CAUSE THEMES FROM TRAINING PERSPECTIVE
Theme 1: Completion bias
- Training program weighted completion and test scores more heavily than real-world behavior under pressure.

Theme 2: Scenario mismatch
- Existing simulations did not sufficiently reproduce real dispatch urgency and authority-gradient dynamics.

Theme 3: Follow-up execution gap
- Observation audit findings did not always trigger timely coaching or risk escalation.

Theme 4: Cross-functional handoff blind spots
- Maintenance training lacked explicit accountability for closure linkage with quality clearance completion.
