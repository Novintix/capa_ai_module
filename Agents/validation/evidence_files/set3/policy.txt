INTRODUCTION
This compendium consolidates policies for deviation management, documentation integrity, restart controls, handover governance, CAPA effectiveness, training, change control, risk escalation, and quality-critical IT governance in implantable device manufacturing.

POLICY 1 - DEVIATION MANAGEMENT AND ESCALATION
Policy ID: POL-QA-005
Requirements:
1. Any departure from approved process or control is a deviation.
2. Deviations are recorded in real time.
3. Critical restart control gaps require immediate QA notification.
4. Major/Critical deviations require CAPA initiation within 5 business days.
5. Repeat deviations trigger management escalation and effectiveness review.

POLICY 2 - GOOD DOCUMENTATION PRACTICE
Policy ID: POL-QA-012
Requirements:
1. Entries must be contemporaneous.
2. Backdating is prohibited.
3. Corrections require documented GDP method.
4. Blank mandatory fields are noncompliant.
5. Verbal claims without records are not acceptable evidence.

POLICY 3 - DATA INTEGRITY GOVERNANCE
Policy ID: POL-QA-017
Requirements:
1. Electronic audit trails remain enabled.
2. Shared credentials are prohibited.
3. Delayed entries require supervisory justification.
4. Data review includes exception trends and outliers.
5. Reconstructed records are integrity events unless formally justified.

POLICY 4 - POST-MAINTENANCE RESTART CONTROL
Policy ID: POL-MDT-041
Requirements:
1. No restart before sterile-path clearance completion.
2. Witness sign-off required before line release.
3. Missing witness signature invalidates restart record.
4. Open clearance at handover requires signed transfer and hold.
5. Restart without completed clearance triggers deviation and risk assessment.

POLICY 5 - ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL RESPONSE
Policy ID: POL-ENG-039
Requirements:
1. Humidity and temperature warning excursions require documented decision.
2. Critical-zone environmental drift cannot be treated as informational only.
3. Repeated warning excursions trigger engineering review.

POLICY 6 - SHIFT HANDOVER GOVERNANCE
Policy ID: POL-MDT-031
Requirements:
1. Open critical items require signed written transfer.
2. Verbal-only transfer is prohibited.
3. Incoming shift must accept ownership in writing.
4. Unassigned open critical item is a control failure.

POLICY 7 - INVESTIGATION AND ROOT CAUSE QUALITY
Policy ID: POL-QA-021
Requirements:
1. Investigation must include timeline and evidence matrix.
2. Conclusions must be evidence-based and testable.
3. At least two independent evidence sources required when feasible.
4. Interim conclusions must be clearly labeled.

POLICY 8 - CAPA GOVERNANCE
Policy ID: POL-QA-022
Requirements:
1. CAPA includes root cause, owner, due date, and measurable effectiveness criteria.
2. Effectiveness checks require outcome verification, not activity completion only.
3. Delayed CAPA on high-risk issues requires formal risk acceptance.
4. Recurrence within 12 months triggers CAPA redesign review.

POLICY 9 - TRAINING AND QUALIFICATION
Policy ID: POL-HR-QMS-014
Requirements:
1. Qualification required before independent task execution.
2. Annual refresher for high-criticality SOPs.
3. Behavioral effectiveness measured through observation audits.
4. Overdue critical modules require supervisory escalation.

POLICY 10 - CHANGE CONTROL
Policy ID: POL-QA-028
Requirements:
1. Quality-impacting changes require risk assessment.
2. Temporary changes need expiry and rollback controls.
3. SOP, forms, and training updates must be synchronized.
4. Pre-implementation training completion mandatory.

POLICY 11 - LOT RECORD REVIEW
Policy ID: POL-QA-034
Requirements:
1. Critical checklist: signatures, timestamps, maintenance links, holds, deviations.
2. Missing critical records block release recommendation.
3. Late entries are reviewed for integrity risk.

POLICY 12 - RISK MANAGEMENT AND ESCALATION
Policy ID: POL-QA-040
Requirements:
1. Potential patient-impact events are escalated immediately.
2. Risk scoring includes severity, occurrence, detectability.
3. Interim controls are documented and tracked to closure.

POLICY 13 - MANAGEMENT REVIEW GOVERNANCE
Policy ID: POL-QA-050
Requirements:
1. Monthly review includes CAPA status, deviation trends, and overdue actions.
2. Open actions tiered by residual risk.
3. Medium risk overdue >30 days requires QA Director risk acceptance.
4. High risk overdue requires Site Director notification within 5 business days.
5. Status-only reporting without risk decision is unacceptable for overdue critical actions.

POLICY 14 - QUALITY-CRITICAL IT PROJECT GOVERNANCE
Policy ID: POL-IT-QMS-015
Requirements:
1. Quality-critical IT controls are listed in formal register.
2. Priority changes require QA approval before effect.
3. Delay beyond committed date requires risk acceptance document.
4. Compensating controls must be monitored until deployment.

POLICY 15 - KPI GOVERNANCE AND COMPLIANCE ALIGNMENT
Policy ID: POL-OPS-QMS-022
Requirements:
1. Shift scorecard includes minimum 25 percent compliance weighting.
2. Correct compliance holds are positive performance actions.
3. Throughput-only scoring is not permitted for high-criticality lines.
4. Annual KPI review by Site Quality Council is mandatory.

POLICY 16 - CAPA EFFECTIVENESS STANDARDS
Policy ID: POL-QA-022-EFF
Requirements:
1. Effectiveness protocols must verify technical control deployment status.
2. Residual-risk notes require explicit management action.
3. Failed effectiveness checks require immediate extension or redesign.
4. Closure decision requires evidence package with outcome metrics.
