STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Title: Controlled Sterile-Path Clearance and Restart After Critical Maintenance
SOP Number: SOP-MDT-041
Version: 3.2
Effective Date: 2026-01-15
Supersedes: SOP-MDT-041 v3.1
Department: Implantable Device Manufacturing Operations

1. PURPOSE
Define mandatory controls to ensure sterile-path readiness and documentation integrity after maintenance that affects implantable device fluid pathways.

2. SCOPE
Applies to all implantable infusion controller lines where maintenance intervention disturbs fluid-path, weld, or sterile-contact boundaries.

3. REFERENCES
- POL-QA-005 Deviation Management
- POL-QA-012 Good Documentation Practice
- POL-MDT-031 Shift Handover Governance
- FRM-MDT-041 Sterile-Path Clearance Form
- FRM-MDT-051 Shift Handover Record
- FRM-QA-DEV-02 Device Deviation Form

4. DEFINITIONS
- Sterile-Path Clearance: Formal verification that post-maintenance pathway is clean, intact, and documented.
- Witness: Authorized QA or qualified delegate verifying clearance completion.
- Hold State: Mandatory run-stop status pending control closure.

5. ROLE RESPONSIBILITIES
5.1 Operator
- Execute checklist in real time.
- Document all outcomes contemporaneously.
- Do not restart line without completed witness sign-off.

5.2 Maintenance Technician
- Complete mechanical intervention and document closure.
- Confirm no tools, temporary parts, or residues remain.
- Verify linkage to FRM-MDT-041 before work order closeout.

5.3 QA Witness
- Verify checklist evidence and physical condition.
- Sign witness field before release.

5.4 Shift Supervisor
- Ensure no open critical item at restart or handover.
- Enforce hold when critical documentation is incomplete.

6. TRIGGER EVENTS REQUIRING FULL CLEARANCE
- Ultrasonic weld horn replacement
- Fluid-path tubing replacement or rerouting
- Valve block replacement in dosing pathway
- Sterile manifold disassembly
- Any maintenance opening controlled sterile boundary

7. PRE-RESTART CONTROL FLOW
Step 1: Set line to HOLD.
Step 2: Confirm maintenance closure details complete.
Step 3: Complete FRM-MDT-041 physical checks.
Step 4: Record timestamps and operator initials.
Step 5: Obtain QA witness signature.
Step 6: Supervisor confirms checklist attached in active lot packet.
Step 7: Release HOLD and resume.

8. CHECKLIST EXECUTION DETAIL
8.1 Cleanliness and debris control
- Confirm no lint, fibers, polymer dust, or residues in critical zones.

8.2 Fluid-path integrity
- Confirm seals, fittings, and clip points are secure and undamaged.

8.3 Documentation completeness
- Confirm current revision form and mandatory fields complete.

8.4 Alarm and environment checks
- Confirm no unresolved critical alarms.
- Confirm humidity and temperature in approved range before restart.

9. DOCUMENTATION RULES
- Entries must be contemporaneous.
- Blank mandatory fields are not acceptable.
- Corrections must follow GDP-compliant strike-through method.
- Delayed entry requires reason, supervisor approval, and QA review.

10. SHIFT HANDOVER REQUIREMENT
If clearance remains open at handover:
- Line remains HOLD unless documented QA exception.
- Open item transferred on signed FRM-MDT-051.
- Incoming shift signs explicit ownership.

11. DEVIATION TRIGGERS
Immediate deviation required when:
- Restart occurs before witness sign-off.
- Open critical item is transferred verbally only.
- Mandatory form fields are reconstructed after the fact.
- Environmental warning band is exceeded and operation continues without documented risk decision.

12. CONTAINMENT GUIDANCE
- Quarantine affected lot units from noncompliant window.
- Increase flow and particulate inspection scope.
- Stop comparable interventions until team briefing if trend recurrence exists.

13. TRAINING REQUIREMENTS
- Initial qualification before independent execution.
- Annual refresher for all high-criticality modules.
- Scenario-based escalation training for operators and supervisors.
- Post-deviation retraining within 10 business days for involved personnel.

14. EFFECTIVENESS METRICS
- 100 percent compliant post-maintenance restarts with witness before RUN.
- Zero verbal-only transfer of open critical items for 3 consecutive months.
- 90 percent reduction in delayed deviation initiation over two quarters.
- Rolling lot trend: low-dose failure rate below alert limit.

15. MES SYSTEM LOCK REQUIREMENT
15.1 Required behavior
- MES must block HOLD to RUN transition when FRM-MDT-041 witness field is blank.
- Applies to all lots linked to critical maintenance work order types.

15.2 Override control
- Override requires dual QA director approval and automatic deviation ticket creation.

15.3 Implementation status
- CR-IT-2025-118 is quality-critical; deployment tracked monthly.
- Until live, compensating controls remain mandatory and high-risk.

16. ESCALATION MATRIX
Scenario A: Witness unavailable at planned restart.
- Action: Delay restart and escalate after 30 minutes to QA manager.

Scenario B: Restart before witness discovered post-hoc.
- Action: Immediate HOLD, deviation within 30 minutes, QA manager notified within 1 hour.

Scenario C: Open item at handover.
- Action: signed transfer required; no verbal-only carry-forward.

Scenario D: Repeated noncompliance pattern.
- Action: CAPA review initiated within 5 business days.

17. TRAINING EFFECTIVENESS RULES
- Completion data alone is insufficient.
- 90-day behavioral observation audit required after refresher.
- Compliance under pressure scenarios must be explicitly tested.
- Any observed score below 90 percent requires coaching and reassessment.

18. MANAGEMENT REVIEW INTEGRATION
- Restart compliance appears as standing agenda item in monthly quality review.
- Two failures in 30 days trigger site leadership briefing.
- CAPA effectiveness must verify technical control deployment, not only training completion.

19. KPI ALIGNMENT REQUIREMENT
- Throughput metrics shall not penalize justified compliance holds.
- Compliance actions are tracked as positive quality performance.
- Annual KPI governance review by Site Quality Council required.

20. REVISION HISTORY

END OF SOP
