SECTION 1 - SITE TRAINING GOVERNANCE OVERVIEW
=============================================================================

1.1 Training Governance Framework
NOVINTIX operates a structured competency and qualification system governed by:
- POL-HR-QMS-014 (Training and Qualification)
- POL-QA-012 (Good Documentation Practice)
- POL-QA-022 (CAPA Governance)
- Site Training Standard: TRN-STD-001 v3.1 (effective 2024-01-01)

Training categories:
Cat A - GMP Fundamentals: All site personnel; annual refresher mandatory.
Cat B - Role-Based SOP Qualification: Required before independent task execution; renewed when SOP is revised.
Cat C - Equipment Operation: Practical qualification on specific equipment; includes simulator and live supervised sessions.
Cat D - Regulatory and Compliance: Annual; covers GDP, data integrity, regulatory expectations.
Cat E - Behavioral and Situational: Scenario-based training on pressure, deviation decision-making, escalation culture.
Cat F - Specialist: Microbiology, sterility assurance, stability, particle science; role-specific.

Training delivery modes:
- e-Learning: Online modules with knowledge assessment (pass mark 80 percent).
- Classroom: Instructor-led with attendance register and post-assessment.
- On-the-Job (OJT): Supervised task execution; sign-off by qualified mentor.
- Simulation: Scenario exercises; assessed by QA facilitator.
- Micro-learning: Short reinforcement modules (10-15 min); no formal assessment.

Training record system: LMS (Learning Management System) TrackGMP v5.2.
All completions, scores, and sign-offs recorded electronically. Paper records retained as backup for OJT sign-offs.

1.2 Training Performance Indicators (Site-Wide, 2025)
- Overall training compliance rate (on-time completion): 94.2 percent.
- Critical SOP refresher compliance rate: 97.1 percent.
- Average knowledge assessment score (all roles, all modules): 86.4 percent.
- Observation audit compliance rate (post-training behavioral check): 88.7 percent.
- Number of deviations attributed to training gap (2025): 7 (vs. 11 in 2024; -36 percent improvement).
- Outstanding overdue training items (as of 2025-12-31): 14 individuals, 22 modules.

Key gap identified in 2025 annual training review:

- Root identified in CAPA-2025-0038: Training design was knowledge-focused; scenario-based content addressing real-world pressure scenarios was not included.

SECTION 7 - TRAINING EFFECTIVENESS ANALYSIS
=============================================================================

7.1 Method
Training effectiveness was assessed through three measures:
(a) Knowledge: Post-training assessment score.
(b) Behavior: Observational audit compliance rate (real-time task performance check, 90 days post-training).
(c) Outcome: Deviation and event correlation with training topics.

7.2 Results Summary for FL-02 Team (2024-2025)

Metric                | FY2024 | FY2025 | Target | Trend
Knowledge avg score   | 88.1%  | 87.4%  | >85%  | At target
Behavior audit rate   | 82.3%  | 84.6%  | >90%  | Below target, improving slowly
Outcome (deviations)  | 11     | 7      | <5    | Reducing but above target

7.3 Critical Findings
Finding T-01: Knowledge-behavior gap is persistent.
- Assessment scores are above target. Behavioral compliance is below target.
- Gap is most pronounced for restart governance (knowledge 91 percent; behavior 78 percent).
- Interpretation: Personnel know the rule but do not reliably apply it when under real conditions.

Finding T-02: Schedule-pressure scenarios are absent from existing training design.
- All current scenario-based modules use neutral (no-pressure) process environments.
- No module recreates the experience of a planning urgency communication from a senior leader.
- No module addresses the operator's authority and obligation to hold the line independently of QA presence.
- This design gap was first identified in CAPA-2025-0038 (March 2025); scenario content was in authoring at time of batch event (November 2025).

Finding T-03: Mentor-transmitted behavioral norms.
- R. Singh was trained by J. Thomas. Both exhibit the same deferred-documentation behavioral pattern.
- Mentor qualification does not currently include behavioral compliance assessment.
- Minimum 2 other operator pairs on site share the same mentor-mentee deferred-documentation pattern (identified in 2025 observation audit program).

Finding T-04: Overdue module tracking lacked escalation.
- Three overdue high-criticality simulation modules on FL-02 team were visible in LMS for >4 weeks before batch event.
- LMS generates automated reminder emails but does not alert QA Manager or Shift Supervisor.
- LMS escalation rules: First reminder at 7 days overdue; second at 30 days. No QA alert at any stage.
- Improvement required: LMS configured to alert QA Manager when high-criticality module is >14 days overdue.

Finding T-05: Effectiveness assessment methodology gap.
- Training effectiveness reports are generated at 90 days post-training for behavioral audit.
- Reports go to Training Coordinator; not routed to QA Manager unless requested.
- QA Manager has no automatic visibility into below-target behavioral audit results.
- Improvement required: Below-threshold behavioral audit results route automatically to QA Manager and are reviewed at next management review.

7.4 Proposed Training Program Changes (arising from event analysis)
1. Develop and deploy "Compliance Under Pressure" simulation module for operators and supervisors; include: senior manager urgency email scenario, QA witness unavailability decision point, and verbal handover temptation scenario.
2. Add behavioral compliance score as mandatory competency gate for mentor designation.
3. Route LMS high-criticality overdue alerts to QA Manager at 14 days.
4. Route below-threshold behavioral audit results (<90 percent) to QA Manager automatically.
5. Include "compliance stop authority" as a standalone qualification element for supervisors.
6. Add maintenance-operator interface training for maintenance technicians on FRM-MFG-022 initiation obligation.
7. Annual training effectiveness review to use outcome-based (deviation rate correlated with training topic) metrics, not only completion and score metrics.

- Route below-90-percent behavioral audit to QA Manager automatically.
- Add batch-assignment pre-check: LMS flags operators with overdue critical modules before batch start.
- Add mentor-mentee behavioral correlation report in annual training effectiveness review.
- Implement knowledge-decay refresh trigger: modules older than 18 months in high-criticality category flagged for reassessment.
