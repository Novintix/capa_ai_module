INTRODUCTION
This compendium consolidates policies applicable to sterile device assembly quality governance, including deviation management, documentation standards, traceability controls, handover governance, CAPA, training, and project governance for quality-critical digital controls.

POLICY 1 - DEVIATION MANAGEMENT
Policy ID: POL-QA-MD-005
Requirements:
1. Any departure from approved procedure is a deviation.
2. Critical deviations require same-shift initiation.
3. Restart without complete release controls requires immediate HOLD and QA notification.
4. Repeat deviations trigger CAPA review.

POLICY 2 - GOOD DOCUMENTATION PRACTICE
Policy ID: POL-QA-MD-012
Requirements:
1. Entries are contemporaneous and attributable.
2. Backdating is prohibited.
3. Blank mandatory fields are non-compliant.
4. Verbal claims without records are not evidence.

POLICY 3 - TRACEABILITY GOVERNANCE
Policy ID: POL-QA-MD-017
Requirements:
1. Component lots must be reconciled before RUN.
2. Unresolved lot-mapping warnings block release authorization.
3. Any warning acknowledgment requires documented corrective action.
4. Traceability audit trail must remain enabled.

POLICY 4 - POST-MAINTENANCE RESTART CONTROL
Policy ID: POL-OPS-MD-044
Requirements:
1. No restart before FRM-ASM-109 completion and witness sign-off.
2. No restart with unresolved traceability warnings.
3. Missing witness signature invalidates release record.
4. Non-compliant restart triggers deviation and risk assessment.

POLICY 5 - WARNING/ALARM MANAGEMENT
Policy ID: POL-ENG-MD-031
Requirements:
1. Acknowledgment is not closure.
2. Repeated warnings require escalation.
3. Product-risk warnings require documented action and supervisor review.

POLICY 6 - SHIFT HANDOVER GOVERNANCE
Policy ID: POL-OPS-MD-031
Requirements:
1. Open critical items must be transferred in signed written form.
2. Verbal-only transfer is prohibited.
3. Incoming shift explicitly accepts ownership in writing.

POLICY 7 - INVESTIGATION STANDARDS
Policy ID: POL-QA-MD-021
Requirements:
1. Investigation includes timeline, evidence matrix, and cause analysis.
2. Conclusions require corroborative evidence where feasible.
3. Interim conclusions are labeled and updated upon final data.

POLICY 8 - CAPA GOVERNANCE
Policy ID: POL-QA-MD-022
Requirements:
1. CAPA includes owner, due date, measurable effectiveness criteria.
2. Effectiveness cannot rely only on activity completion.
3. Delayed CAPA requires formal risk acceptance.
4. Recurrence within 12 months triggers CAPA redesign review.

POLICY 9 - TRAINING AND QUALIFICATION
Policy ID: POL-HR-MD-014
Requirements:
1. Qualification required before independent execution.
2. Annual refresher for critical SOPs.
3. Behavioral effectiveness monitored via observation audits.
4. Sub-threshold audits require mandatory follow-up action.

POLICY 10 - CHANGE CONTROL
Policy ID: POL-QA-MD-028
Requirements:
1. Changes affecting fluid-path geometry/risk require formal change control.
2. Related SOP/forms/training updates synchronized before go-live.
3. Temporary changes require expiry and rollback plan.

POLICY 11 - DHR REVIEW AND RELEASE SUPPORT
Policy ID: POL-QA-MD-034
Requirements:
1. Mandatory completeness check for signatures, warnings, interventions, deviations.
2. Missing critical records prevent release recommendation.
3. Late critical entries reviewed for integrity risk.

POLICY 12 - RISK MANAGEMENT
Policy ID: POL-QA-MD-040
Requirements:
1. Risk scoring considers severity, occurrence, detectability.
2. Interim controls tracked to closure.
3. Potential patient impact escalated immediately.

POLICY 13 - MANAGEMENT REVIEW GOVERNANCE
Policy ID: POL-QA-MD-050
Requirements:
1. Monthly review must include CAPA delays and residual risk tiering.
2. High-risk overdue items require formal escalation and risk acceptance.
3. Status labels alone are not acceptable for aging high-risk controls.

POLICY 14 - QUALITY-CRITICAL IT PROJECT GOVERNANCE
Policy ID: POL-IT-MD-015
Requirements:
1. Quality-critical digital controls must be listed in a governed register.
2. Priority changes require QA approval and documented impact.
3. Delay beyond committed date requires risk acceptance within 15 business days.
4. Compensating controls monitored monthly.

POLICY 15 - KPI GOVERNANCE ALIGNMENT
Policy ID: POL-OPS-MD-022
Requirements:
1. Shift scorecards include compliance weighting at minimum 25 percent.
2. Correct compliance holds are scored as positive quality behavior.
3. KPI changes require compliance impact assessment.

POLICY 16 - CAPA EFFECTIVENESS STANDARD
Policy ID: POL-QA-MD-022-EFF
Requirements:
1. Technical-control CAPAs require deployment verification evidence.
2. Residual risk requires tier assignment and approval when medium/high.
3. Recurrence triggers retrospective effectiveness-method review.

POLICY 17 - QA WITNESS PRE-BOOKING
Policy ID: POL-QA-MD-WIT-001
Requirements:
1. QA witness pre-assigned 2 hours before planned post-maintenance restart.
2. If witness unavailable >30 minutes, restart delayed and escalated.
3. Planning log must show witness assignment status.

POLICY 18 - IN-PROCESS ELEVATED-RISK MONITORING
Policy ID: POL-QC-MD-IPC-020
Requirements:
1. Elevated-risk restart events require enhanced visual and flow checks.
2. Triggered in-process checks documented in DHR.
3. Action-limit exceedance requires immediate HOLD.

POLICY 19 - EQUIPMENT QUALIFICATION
Policy ID: POL-ENG-MD-QUA-021
Requirements:
1. Post-maintenance verification required before release.
2. Guide/routing geometry changes require risk assessment.
3. Qualification plans include particulate-shed risk.

POLICY 20 - HUMAN FACTORS GOVERNANCE
Policy ID: POL-HR-MD-HF-022
Requirements:
1. Critical decision points must be designed for low ambiguity.
2. Urgency communications should include compliance-first language.
3. Mentor eligibility requires behavioral compliance screening.

APPENDIX A - POLICY CROSSWALK

APPENDIX B - ENFORCEMENT PRINCIPLES
- Repeat findings are systemic indicators.
- Exceptions require formal approval with time-bound closure plan.
- Patient-risk relevant deviations receive highest escalation priority.
