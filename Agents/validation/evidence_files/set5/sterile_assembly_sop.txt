STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Title: Post-Maintenance Release Control for Sterile Fluid-Path Device Assembly
SOP Number: SOP-ASM-061
Version: 3.2
Effective Date: 2026-01-15
Department: MedTech Manufacturing Operations

1. PURPOSE
Define mandatory controls for safe and compliant restart after maintenance activities that affect sterile fluid-path assembly lines.

2. SCOPE
Applies to all assembly lines producing infusion sets, extension lines, and other sterile single-use devices where maintenance can affect fluid-path integrity, traceability, or particulate risk.

3. REFERENCES
- POL-QA-MD-005 Deviation Management
- POL-QA-MD-012 Good Documentation Practice
- POL-OPS-MD-031 Shift Handover Governance
- FRM-ASM-109 Post-Maintenance Release Form
- FRM-OPS-041 Shift Handover Form
- FRM-QA-DEV-07 MedTech Deviation Form

4. DEFINITIONS
- Post-Maintenance Release: Required pre-RUN verification sequence after maintenance intervention.
- Witness: Authorized QA personnel validating release criteria.
- DHR: Device History Record for the batch.
- Lot Reconciliation: Verification that scanned component lots match approved BOM mapping.

5. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
5.1 Operator
- Complete FRM-ASM-109 in real time.
- Verify lot mapping status is green before restart.
- Do not restart without witness sign-off.

5.2 Maintenance Technician
- Complete intervention tasks and mechanical checks.
- Confirm no temporary tools/components remain.
- Trigger QA notification workflow prior to area release.

5.3 QA Witness
- Verify physical and documentation controls.
- Confirm traceability warning status is resolved.
- Sign witness field prior to restart.

5.4 Shift Supervisor
- Enforce HOLD if any critical release field is incomplete.
- Ensure open critical items are transferred in signed written form.

6. TRIGGER EVENTS REQUIRING FULL RELEASE CONTROL
- Manifold/nozzle replacement
- Tubing lane guide replacement
- Fluid-path seal replacement
- Any intervention opening sterile boundary
- Any maintenance event changing lot-scanning path configuration

7. PRE-RESTART CONTROL FLOW
Step 1: Set line to HOLD.
Step 2: Verify maintenance completion signature.
Step 3: Complete FRM-ASM-109 physical checks.
Step 4: Confirm lot reconciliation status is green for all lanes.
Step 5: QA witness verifies and signs.
Step 6: Supervisor verifies DHR attachment completeness.
Step 7: Release HOLD and restart.

8. REQUIRED CHECKLIST ITEMS
8.1 Physical integrity checks
- No debris in tubing channels or connector interfaces.
- Guide inserts and clips intact with no abrasion edges.
- UV manifold seated and leak-free.

8.2 Traceability checks
- All lots scanned and mapped to active DHR.
- No unresolved traceability warnings.

8.3 Documentation checks
- FRM-ASM-109 complete, no blank mandatory fields.
- Timestamped and attributable entries only.

8.4 Alarm status checks
- No unresolved alarms linked to restart area.

9. DOCUMENTATION RULES
- All entries must be contemporaneous.
- Backfilling critical fields is non-compliant and requires deviation.
- Corrections must follow GDP requirements.

10. SHIFT HANDOVER RULES
- Open critical item requires signed FRM-OPS-041 transfer.
- Verbal-only transfer is prohibited.
- If signed transfer unavailable, line remains HOLD.

11. DEVIATION TRIGGERS
Immediate deviation is required when:
- Restart occurs before witness sign-off.
- Restart occurs with unresolved lot-reconciliation warning.
- Critical release item transferred verbally only.
- Critical field reconstructed post-facto.

12. IMMEDIATE CONTAINMENT GUIDANCE
- HOLD affected line.
- Quarantine units produced during non-compliant interval.
- Increase visual and flow verification sampling.
- Notify QA manager within 60 minutes.

13. TRAINING REQUIREMENTS
- Initial qualification before independent operation.
- Annual refresher for all affected roles.
- Scenario training covering stop authority under schedule pressure.
- 90-day post-training observation audit with follow-up for <90 percent compliance.

14. EFFECTIVENESS METRICS
- 100 percent witness sign-off before RUN on maintenance-triggered restarts.
- Zero unresolved lot warnings at restart.
- Zero verbal-only critical handovers.
- Monthly trend review at MedTech Quality Review Board.

15. MES LOCK REQUIREMENT
15.1 Required behavior
- Prevent HOLD to RUN transition when FRM-ASM-109 witness field is blank.
- Prevent HOLD to RUN transition if lot reconciliation warning unresolved.
- Any override requires dual QA leadership approval and auto-deviation creation.

15.2 Implementation status
- Change request CR-IT-2025-188 specified in 2025.
- Target go-live revised to 2026-Q2.
- Until deployment, manual compensating controls remain mandatory.

16. ESCALATION MATRIX
Scenario A: Witness unavailable
- Delay restart and escalate to QA duty manager after 30 minutes.

Scenario B: Restart already occurred without complete controls
- Immediate HOLD, deviation initiation, QA manager notification, risk assessment.

Scenario C: Open critical item at handover
- Mandatory written transfer; no written transfer means HOLD.

Scenario D: Recurrence trend
- CAPA review within five business days.

17. POST-MAINTENANCE VERIFICATION (PMV) DETAILS
PMV-1: Physical inspection of fluid-path components.
PMV-2: Flush and flow pre-check.
PMV-3: Commissioning run and alarm check.
PMV-4: QA witness confirmation.
Minimum planned PMV window: 30 to 40 minutes.

18. IN-PROCESS ENHANCED CHECKS AFTER RESTART
- First 200 units: enhanced visual inspection.
- 15-minute flow verification for first 2 hours.
- Immediate HOLD if particulate or flow anomaly exceeds limits.

19. ALARM RESPONSE PRINCIPLE
Alarm acknowledgment is not corrective action. Documented response and, when required, escalation are mandatory.

20. CHANGE CONTROL LINKAGE
Any routing, guide insert, or support geometry change affecting fluid-path risk requires formal change control and QA approval.
