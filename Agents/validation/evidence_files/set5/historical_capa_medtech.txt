SYSTEMIC TREND SUMMARY
Trend A: Restart control failures cluster near maintenance plus schedule pressure windows.
Trend B: Verbal handover recurrence despite policy reminders.
Trend C: Effectiveness reviews over-rely on activity completion.
Trend D: Technical-control deployment delays not consistently risk-accepted.
Trend E: KPI design can discourage compliance-first stop decisions.

LESSONS FOR CURRENT INVESTIGATION
1. Manual compensating controls are fragile when schedule pressure is high.
2. Handover controls require system-backed enforcement, not policy alone.
3. Technical controls must be verified as deployed before CAPA effectiveness closure.
4. Management review must be risk-tiered, not status-only.
