QUALITY POLICY
Title     : Deviation Management and Escalation Policy
Policy No : POL-QA-005
Version   : 2.0
Effective : 2023-09-01
Approved  : Dr. A. Kumar (QA Director)

-----------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
To define site-wide expectations for identifying, documenting, escalating, and resolving
deviations from approved procedures, specifications, and process parameters.

-----------------------------------------------------------
2. SCOPE
All manufacturing, quality control, and support functions at this facility.

-----------------------------------------------------------
3. POLICY STATEMENTS

3.1 Any departure from an approved procedure or specified parameter range constitutes a
    deviation and must be documented.

3.2 Operators are responsible for identifying and reporting deviations in real time.
    Deviations must not be concealed, minimized, or closed without appropriate review.

3.3 Critical process parameters (CPPs) are defined in product-specific batch records.
    Excursion beyond a CPP limit requires immediate process hold and QA notification.

3.4 Equipment alarms for CPP-related sensors (temperature, pressure, speed) must not be
    acknowledged and dismissed without investigation. Acknowledging an alarm does not
    constitute corrective action.

3.5 Shift supervisors are accountable for ensuring deviations occurring during their shift
    are documented on Deviation Form QA-DEV-01 before shift end.

3.6 Shift handover must be performed in writing using Form MFG-HO-03. Verbal handovers
    are not permitted as the sole record for a shift. Written records must capture any
    open alarms, parameter excursions, or pending actions.

3.7 All deviations must be assigned a risk classification:
      - Critical : Potential direct impact on product quality or patient safety
      - Major    : Potential indirect impact; investigation mandatory
      - Minor    : Low risk; documentation and monitoring sufficient

3.8 A CAPA must be initiated for all Critical and Major deviations within 5 business days
    of identification.

-----------------------------------------------------------
4. CONSEQUENCES OF NON-COMPLIANCE
Failure to follow this policy may result in:
  - Batch rejection or quarantine
  - Regulatory reporting obligations
  - Personnel retraining or disciplinary action
  - Repeat audit findings
