QUALITY, FUNCTIONAL RELIABILITY, AND PARTICULATE ASSESSMENT REPORT
Report Number: QFR-MDT-2026-012
Product: Single-Use Infusion Pump Cartridge Set 10 mL (Sterile)
Batch: IPC-117A
Legacy Evidence Tag: MT-117
Issued By: QC and Reliability Sciences Group
Issue Date: 2026-03-01

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Batch IPC-117A showed elevated visual particulate rejects in sterile fluid pathway chambers. Functional flow and pressure performance remained within specification for non-defective samples. The strongest correlation is with process-governance failure around post-maintenance restart and delayed control closure.

2. PURPOSE
Consolidate quality, functional, and particulate evidence for investigation and CAPA decisions.

3. SAMPLE PLAN AND METHODS
3.1 Sampling Strategy
- Final visual rejects and acceptances sampled across beginning, middle, and end of run.
- Retained samples selected from three run segments.
3.2 Visual and Microscopy Evaluation
- Controlled booth visual inspection.
- Stereomicroscopy and SEM-EDS for particle characterization.
3.3 Functional Reliability Tests
- Flow delivery accuracy at simulated use rates.
- Back-pressure tolerance and leak integrity tests.
- Connector fit and luer retention checks.
3.4 Ancillary Evidence Review
- Assembly timeline cross-check with logbook and MES event trail.

4. RESULTS
4.1 Visual Defect Findings
- Rejected units: 186 out of 12,000 (1.55 percent).
- Alert limit: 0.6 percent; action limit: 0.9 percent.
- Defect trend was highest in final third of run.

4.2 Functional Performance (non-defective sample subset)
- Flow rate accuracy: Within specification.
- Back-pressure tolerance: Within specification.
- Leak integrity: Within specification.
- Conclusion: Core mechanical performance intact where particulate absent.

4.3 Trend Context
- Prior four lots on line AS-04 remained at or below 0.42 percent rejects.
- IPC-117A reject rate is statistically elevated and operationally distinct.

5. PARTICULATE MORPHOLOGY SUMMARY
- Predominant morphology: translucent filamentous fibers, 80 to 310 micrometers length.
- Secondary morphology: short opaque fragments, 20 to 70 micrometers.
- Interpretation: likely extrinsic polymer source, not formulation residue.

6. CORRELATION WITH PROCESS EVIDENCE
- Maintenance-triggered restart occurred before complete witness sign-off.
- Open compliance item was transferred verbally at shift boundary.
- Throughput acceleration occurred during period of incomplete control documentation.
- No environmental monitoring excursions support ambient contamination source.

7. RISK INTERPRETATION
Quality Risk: Elevated
Compliance Risk: Elevated
Patient Use Risk: Medium-High pending full disposition
Clinical Consequence Context: Potential for infusion pathway occlusion or inflammatory response if particulate enters administration circuit.

8. RECOMMENDATIONS
Immediate:
1. Maintain quarantine for all IPC-117A units.
2. Continue 100 percent visual and sub-visible particulate reinspection.
3. Conduct replacement and verification of line AS-04 guide rail sleeve components.
Near-Term:
1. Implement MES lock for witness-dependent restart gating.
2. Enforce signed written handover for unresolved critical items.
3. Open systemic CAPA on governance and KPI alignment.

9. LIMITATIONS
- Final source attribution confidence is high but not absolute until full material exclusion panel completed.
- Some timeline evidence is logbook narrative requiring audit-trail corroboration.

10. CONCLUSION
Event profile supports process-execution and governance failure around post-maintenance restart controls, with particulate source most consistent with polymer shed from worn guide rail sleeve near tubing feed path. Functional mechanics were not the primary failure mode.
