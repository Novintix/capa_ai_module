STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Title: Controlled Line Readiness and Restart After Product-Contact Maintenance Interventions
SOP Number: SOP-MDT-058
Version: 3.4
Effective Date: 2026-01-15
Supersedes: SOP-MDT-058 v3.3
Department: Manufacturing Operations - Medical Device Assembly

1. PURPOSE
Define mandatory controls to ensure sterile assembly lines are safe and compliant for restart after maintenance affecting product-contact or product-contact-adjacent pathways.

2. SCOPE
Applies to all sterile assembly lines where maintenance opens, repositions, replaces, or disturbs components that may affect fluid-path cleanliness, seal integrity, or particulate risk.

3. REFERENCES
- POL-QA-005 Deviation Management and Escalation
- POL-QA-012 Good Documentation Practice
- POL-MFG-031 Shift Handover Governance
- FRM-MDT-041 Line Readiness Checklist
- FRM-MFG-031 Shift Handover Form
- FRM-QA-DEV-01 Deviation Record

4. DEFINITIONS
- Line Readiness: Verified state in which line can restart after maintenance with documented evidence and QA witness confirmation.
- Witness: Authorized QA professional who verifies checklist completion and physical line condition.
- Hold State: Status where operation cannot continue until release criteria are met.

5. ROLE RESPONSIBILITIES
5.1 Operator
- Execute checklist in real time.
- Record findings contemporaneously.
- Do not initiate RUN before witness sign-off.

5.2 Maintenance Technician
- Confirm intervention complete and tools/components accounted for.
- Ensure work order includes handoff to operations and readiness checklist initiation status.

5.3 QA Witness
- Verify physical line condition and checklist completion.
- Sign witness field before line release.

5.4 Shift Supervisor
- Enforce hold if any critical field is incomplete.
- Ensure written handover for unresolved critical items.

6. TRIGGER EVENTS REQUIRING FULL LINE READINESS CHECK
- Ultrasonic weld horn replacement.
- Tubing feed guide replacement or realignment.
- Product-path connector replacement.
- Valve or manifold service affecting fluid path.
- Any intervention that may increase particulate shed risk.

7. PRE-RESTART CONTROL FLOW
Step 1: Set line status to HOLD.
Step 2: Verify maintenance closure and intervention summary.
Step 3: Perform physical line readiness checks using FRM-MDT-041.
Step 4: Complete operator timestamped checklist entries.
Step 5: Obtain QA witness sign-off.
Step 6: Supervisor verifies readiness form in active batch packet.
Step 7: Change line status from HOLD to RUN.

8. CHECKLIST EXECUTION DETAIL
8.1 Cleanliness and Particulate Risk
- Confirm no visible fibers, residues, or loose fragments in product-contact and adjacent movement paths.
- Confirm protective sleeves and guide interfaces are intact.

8.2 Product-Contact Path Integrity
- Confirm connections, seals, and tubing alignment.
- Confirm no temporary fixtures remain in critical path.

8.3 Documentation Completeness
- Confirm current form revision.
- Confirm no blank mandatory fields.

8.4 Alarm and Status Review
- Confirm no unresolved alarms linked to maintained station.

9. DOCUMENTATION RULES
- Entries must be contemporaneous.
- Blank mandatory fields are non-compliant.
- Corrections must follow GDP controls.
- Delayed entries require reason, time, and supervisor acknowledgment.

10. SHIFT HANDOVER REQUIREMENT
If readiness checklist remains open at shift boundary:
- Line remains HOLD unless QA grants documented exception.
- Open item must be transferred via signed FRM-MFG-031.
- Incoming shift must accept ownership in writing.
- Verbal-only transfer is prohibited.

11. DEVIATION TRIGGERS
Immediate deviation required when:
- Restart occurs before witness sign-off.
- Mandatory readiness records are missing in active packet.
- Open critical item is transferred verbally only.
- Critical control step is reconstructed post-facto.

12. RISK CONTAINMENT GUIDANCE
- Quarantine affected units from uncontrolled restart period.
- Expand particulate and visual checks.
- Pause similar maintenance-restart events for targeted briefing when trend indicates recurrence.

13. TRAINING REQUIREMENTS
- Initial qualification for operators, supervisors, QA witnesses, and maintenance technicians.
- Annual refresher for all roles.
- Scenario-based module required for escalation under schedule pressure.

14. EFFECTIVENESS METRICS
- Target: 100 percent restart events with complete witness sign-off before RUN.
- Target: Zero verbal-only transfers of open critical items for three consecutive months.
- Target: No delayed critical entries across six audit cycles.
- Review cadence: Monthly Manufacturing Quality Review.

15. MES SYSTEM LOCK REQUIREMENT
15.1 Requirement
MES must block HOLD to RUN transition when FRM-MDT-041 witness field is blank for any active maintenance-linked batch.

15.2 Override Governance
Any override requires dual approval from QA Director and Manufacturing Director, with automatic deviation creation.

15.3 Implementation Status
ITR-MDT-2025-0314 remains in staged deployment. Until full release, manual controls in Sections 7 and 10 remain mandatory.

16. ESCALATION MATRIX
Scenario A - Witness unavailable at planned restart:
- Restart delay mandatory.
- Escalate to QA Duty Manager within 30 minutes.

Scenario B - Restart discovered without witness sign-off:
- Immediate HOLD.
- Deviation within 30 minutes.
- QA Manager notification within 1 hour.

Scenario C - Open readiness item at handover:
- Written transfer mandatory.
- If transfer cannot be completed, maintain HOLD and escalate.

Scenario D - Repeated event pattern:
- CAPA trigger within 5 business days.
- Site leadership review within 10 business days.

17. COMPETENCY FRAMEWORK
17.1 Operators
- Classroom SOP qualification and practical supervised restarts.
- Scenario assessment covering schedule pressure decision points.

17.2 Supervisors
- Authority and accountability simulation for compliance holds.
- Annual behavioral calibration with QA.

17.3 Maintenance
- Work order closure plus readiness-link awareness module.
- Confirmation that handoff obligations are complete before departure.

17.4 QA Witness
- Calibration workshops to ensure uniform witness standards.

18. MANAGEMENT REVIEW INTEGRATION
- Restart compliance appears as standing KPI.
- Overdue high-risk actions require formal risk acceptance and escalation.
- Recurrent failures trigger cross-functional governance review.
