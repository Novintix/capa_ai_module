INTRODUCTION
This compendium consolidates policies relevant to deviation management, GDP, data integrity, post-maintenance restart, handover governance, CAPA, training, change control, and management review for sterile medical device assembly operations.

POLICY 1 - DEVIATION MANAGEMENT AND ESCALATION
Policy ID: POL-QA-005
Requirements:
1. Any procedural departure is a deviation.
2. Deviation must be recorded in real time.
3. Restart without completed witness sign-off is an immediate deviation trigger.
4. Major and critical deviations require CAPA initiation within 5 business days.

POLICY 2 - GOOD DOCUMENTATION PRACTICE
Policy ID: POL-QA-012
Requirements:
1. Entries are contemporaneous.
2. Backdating is prohibited.
3. Blank mandatory fields are non-compliant records.
4. Verbal claims without records are not acceptable evidence.

POLICY 3 - DATA INTEGRITY GOVERNANCE
Policy ID: POL-QA-017
Requirements:
1. Audit trails remain enabled in all GMP systems.
2. Shared credentials are prohibited.
3. Delayed entries require documented reason and supervisory acknowledgment.

POLICY 4 - POST-MAINTENANCE RESTART CONTROL
Policy ID: POL-MDT-058
Requirements:
1. No restart before readiness checklist completion and witness sign-off.
2. Missing witness signature invalidates release readiness.
3. Any breach requires immediate line hold and risk assessment.

POLICY 5 - SHIFT HANDOVER GOVERNANCE
Policy ID: POL-MFG-031
Requirements:
1. Open critical items must be transferred in signed written format.
2. Verbal-only transfer is prohibited.
3. Incoming shift must acknowledge ownership.

POLICY 6 - CAPA GOVERNANCE
Policy ID: POL-QA-022
Requirements:
1. CAPA must define measurable effectiveness criteria.
2. Effectiveness verification must confirm control outcomes.
3. Overdue high-risk CAPA requires documented risk acceptance.

POLICY 7 - TRAINING AND QUALIFICATION
Policy ID: POL-HR-QMS-014
Requirements:
1. Role qualification required before independent execution.
2. Annual refresher required for critical SOPs.
3. Behavioral effectiveness monitored via observation audits.

POLICY 8 - CHANGE CONTROL
Policy ID: POL-QA-028
Requirements:
1. Changes affecting particulate risk require formal impact assessment.
2. IT controls affecting quality require QA approval for priority changes.
3. Training must be complete before change effective date.

POLICY 9 - MANAGEMENT REVIEW GOVERNANCE
Policy ID: POL-QA-050
Requirements:
1. Monthly review includes CAPA status, trend signals, and overdue high-risk actions.
2. High-risk overdue items require formal escalation and risk acceptance.
3. Minutes must record decisions and accountability, not status-only updates.

POLICY 10 - QUALITY-CRITICAL IT PROJECT GOVERNANCE
Policy ID: POL-IT-QMS-015
Requirements:
1. Quality-critical IT controls tracked in dedicated register.
2. Priority downgrade requires QA written approval.
3. Delay beyond committed date requires risk acceptance within 15 business days.

POLICY 11 - KPI GOVERNANCE AND COMPLIANCE ALIGNMENT
Policy ID: POL-OPS-QMS-022
Requirements:
1. Shift scorecards must include compliance weighting of at least 25 percent.
2. Validated compliance-hold actions must not be scored as production failure.
3. KPI design changes require compliance impact assessment.

POLICY 12 - MATERIALS AND PARTICULATE RISK MANAGEMENT
Policy ID: POL-QA-MAT-018
Requirements:
1. Product-contact and adjacent movement components require particulate-shed risk review.
2. Any geometry or routing change with shed-risk implication requires change control.
3. Periodic integrity checks required for sleeves, guides, and interfaces in moving feed paths.
