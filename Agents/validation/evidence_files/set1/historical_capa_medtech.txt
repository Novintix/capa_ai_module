TREND ANALYSIS SUMMARY
Trend 1: Handover governance failures appear repeatedly in critical events.
Trend 2: Delayed critical documentation persists under schedule pressure.
Trend 3: Technical controls identified in CAPA are closed administratively before deployment verification.
Trend 4: Production KPI structures have not consistently reinforced compliance-first behavior.

LESSONS LEARNED
1. Human-only controls are insufficient for restart gates.
2. Written handover transfer is an effective barrier only when strictly enforced.
3. CAPA effectiveness must verify control outcomes, not only action completion.
4. IT priority decisions affecting quality controls require formal QA governance.

RECOMMENDED PREVENTIVE CONTROLS

- MES lock with no bypass except dual executive approval and auto-deviation.
- Risk-tier escalation in management review for overdue high-risk actions.
- KPI redesign with compliance-weighted scoring.
