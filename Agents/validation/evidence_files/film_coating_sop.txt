STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Title     : Film Coating Operation for Solid Dosage Forms
SOP No    : SOP-MFG-018
Version   : 3.1
Effective : 2024-06-01
Dept      : Manufacturing

-----------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
To define the standard operating procedure for film coating of tablet cores using
aqueous coating systems in perforated coating pans.

-----------------------------------------------------------
2. SCOPE
Applicable to all film coating operations on Equipment CP-01 through CP-06.

-----------------------------------------------------------
3. EQUIPMENT AND MATERIALS
- Perforated coating pan (capacity 60-120 kg)
- Spray gun assembly
- Coating solution (as per product-specific batch manufacturing record)
- Calibrated thermometer / thermocouple (calibration current within 12 months)

-----------------------------------------------------------
4. PROCESS PARAMETERS (STANDARD RANGES)

Parameter                  | Specification
---------------------------|---------------------------
Inlet air temperature      | 55 C - 60 C
Exhaust air temperature    | 35 C - 42 C
Pan speed                  | 7 - 10 RPM
Spray rate                 | 15 - 25 g/min per gun
Atomization air pressure   | 2.0 - 3.0 bar
Coating solution viscosity | 250 - 300 cP
Tablet bed temp            | 38 C - 45 C

All parameters must remain within specification throughout the coating cycle.
Any deviation from specification must be recorded immediately on the Deviation Form
(Form QA-DEV-01) and escalated to the Shift Supervisor and QA within 30 minutes.

-----------------------------------------------------------
5. OPERATING PROCEDURE

5.1 Pre-Coating
  a. Verify equipment cleanliness and line clearance.
  b. Prepare coating solution per batch record. Check viscosity before use.
  c. Load tablet cores. Record core weight.
  d. Start pan rotation at low speed (5 RPM) during pre-heat only.
  e. Pre-heat pan to target inlet temperature before starting spray.

5.2 Coating
  a. Set pan speed to target value per batch record (within 7-10 RPM range).
  b. Begin spray. Monitor and record all parameters every 30 minutes.
  c. If inlet temperature exceeds 60 C, immediately reduce heat supply and notify supervisor.
  d. Do NOT continue spray during unresolved temperature excursion.
  e. Pan speed must not be reduced below 7 RPM during active coating. If noise or
     mechanical issue occurs, stop the pan and notify maintenance.

5.3 Post-Coating
  a. Stop spray. Continue pan rotation during drying phase at 6-8 RPM.
  b. Discharge tablets when tablet bed temperature drops below 35 C.
  c. Perform visual inspection. Document findings.
  d. Complete batch record before transferring to QC.

5.4 Shift Handover
  a. Outgoing operator MUST complete written handover form (Form MFG-HO-03).
  b. Any deviations, alarms, or parameter excursions occurring during the shift
     MUST be documented on the handover form before shift end.
  c. Verbal-only handovers are NOT acceptable.

-----------------------------------------------------------
6. DEVIATIONS
Any parameter outside stated specification is a deviation and must be handled per
SOP-QA-021 (Deviation and CAPA Management).
