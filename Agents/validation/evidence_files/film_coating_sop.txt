STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Title: Controlled Line Clearance and Clear-Clean-Check After Maintenance Interventions
SOP Number: SOP-MFG-044
Version: 4.3
Effective Date: 2025-03-15
Supersedes: SOP-MFG-044 v4.2
Department: Manufacturing Operations

1. PURPOSE
Define mandatory activities to assure product-contact line readiness after maintenance interventions, and prevent contamination risks associated with incomplete clearance.

2. SCOPE
Applies to all filling, transfer, and product-contact support lines where maintenance opens or disturbs closed process pathways.

3. REFERENCES
- POL-QA-005 Deviation Management and Escalation
- POL-QA-012 Good Documentation Practice
- POL-MFG-031 Shift Handover Governance
- FRM-MFG-022 Clear-Clean-Check
- FRM-MFG-031 Shift Handover Form
- FRM-QA-DEV-01 Deviation Record

4. DEFINITIONS
- Clear-Clean-Check: Structured post-maintenance verification of cleanliness, readiness, and record completeness.
- Witness: Authorized QA or delegate who confirms checklist completion.
- Hold State: Operational lockout where processing cannot continue without release.

5. ROLE RESPONSIBILITIES
5.1 Operator
- Execute checklist in real time.
- Record findings and abnormalities.
- Do not resume processing without completed checklist and witness sign-off.
5.2 Maintenance Technician
- Confirm intervention complete and area free of tools or temporary parts.
- Sign work order closure details.
5.3 QA Witness
- Verify checklist evidence and line condition.
- Sign witness section before release.
5.4 Shift Supervisor
- Confirm no open critical record before shift handover and restart.

6. TRIGGER EVENTS REQUIRING FULL CLEAR-CLEAN-CHECK
- Pump-head replacement
- Product-contact hose disconnection/replacement
- Valve seal replacement in product path
- Nozzle or manifold disassembly
- Any maintenance opening sterile boundary in processing path

7. PRE-RESTART CONTROL FLOW
Step 1: Set line status HOLD.
Step 2: Verify intervention closure with maintenance signature.
Step 3: Perform physical inspection against FRM-MFG-022 sections.
Step 4: Record completion timestamps and operator initials.
Step 5: Obtain QA witness signature.
Step 6: Supervisor verifies attached checklist in batch packet.
Step 7: Release HOLD and resume operation.

8. CHECKLIST EXECUTION DETAIL
8.1 Area and Accessory Cleanliness
- Confirm no lint, residues, fibers, or loose packaging debris.
- Confirm all cleaning labels are current.
8.2 Product-Contact Path Integrity
- Confirm fittings locked and seals seated.
- Confirm no temporary tools or parts remain.
8.3 Documentation Completeness
- Confirm form revision current.
- Confirm mandatory fields completed.
8.4 Alarm and Status Review
- Confirm no active unresolved alarms linked to intervention area.

9. DOCUMENTATION RULES
- All entries must be contemporaneous.
- No blank mandatory fields permitted.
- Corrections require GDP-compliant correction method.
- Delayed entry requires documented reason and supervisor approval.

10. SHIFT HANDOVER REQUIREMENT
If checklist remains open at handover:
- Processing remains HOLD unless QA grants documented exception.
- Open item transferred via signed FRM-MFG-031.
- Incoming shift explicitly accepts ownership.

11. DEVIATION TRIGGERS
Immediate deviation required when any of the following occurs:
- Restart prior to witness sign-off.
- Missing checklist attachment in active batch packet.
- Verbal-only transfer of open critical item.
- Post-facto record reconstruction of critical control step.

12. RISK CONTAINMENT GUIDANCE
- Quarantine affected units where restart control failure occurred.
- Expand visual and particulate inspection as directed by QA.
- Stop similar interventions pending briefing if trend indicates repeat issue.

13. TRAINING REQUIREMENTS
- Initial qualification for all operators and supervisors.
- Annual refresher mandatory.
- Additional briefing after critical/major related deviations.

14. EFFECTIVENESS METRICS
- Percent interventions with complete checklist before restart.
- Number of restart deviations per quarter.
- Number of open critical items transferred at handover.
- Audit observations related to line clearance governance.

15. RECORD RETENTION
- FRM-MFG-022 retained with batch packet per retention schedule.
- Related deviation records retained per QA archival policy.

16. ATTACHMENT - EXAMPLE COMPLETION STANDARD
- Operator field complete
- Witness field complete
- Time stamps in sequence
- No open critical comments without linked deviation number

END OF SOP
